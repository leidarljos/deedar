The tiled log lives in the rfc9162 repository (https://github.com/leidarljos/rfc9162): `rfc9162`, the Merkle tree deedar's log re-exports, and `rfc9162-tiles`, tiled, indexed storage for such a log in the C2SP tlog-tiles layout (256-hash tiles, 256-entry bundles, partial tiles for the tail), a locked writer with torn-tail recovery, a reader that checks every tile against the trusted root and assembles the same proof bytes as the flat crate, per-bundle key sidecars with a split-block Bloom filter and a manifest for `find`, and C2SP checkpoints as Ed25519 signed notes with cosignature/v1 verification.
This workspace depends on `rfc9162` from crates.io and on `rfc9162-tiles` from that repository until its first release; neither crate is a member here any more.
