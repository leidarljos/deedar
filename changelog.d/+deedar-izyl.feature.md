The Merkle log (leaf and node hashes, root, inclusion and consistency proofs, head) is its own crate, `rfc9162`, which deedar re-exports from `deedar::log`. `Head::material` takes the domain tag (`deedar::log::DOMAIN` is `deedar-log-v1`). Signed heads, receipts, bridges and the stored log are the same bytes, and a store written by 0.6.1 verifies unchanged.
