deedar::log
~~~~~~~~~~~

An append-only Merkle log over the deeds a store has issued, hashed as RFC 9162 (doi:10.17487/RFC9162): leaf ``SHA-256(0x00 || entry)``, node ``SHA-256(0x01 || left || right)``. Inclusion proofs catch a dropped deed; consistency proofs catch a rewritten log. Signatures alone bind neither. Gossip lives outside this file. Two consistent logs shown to two readers both verify here.

`rustdoc <rustdoc/deedar/log/index.html>`__

.. rubric:: Structs

- `Entry <rustdoc/deedar/log/struct.Entry.html>`__ (struct). One entry: the deed’s content address and the instant it was logged.
- `Head <rustdoc/deedar/log/struct.Head.html>`__ (struct). What a store publishes: how many entries it has, and the root over them.

.. rubric:: Functions

- `consistency_proof <rustdoc/deedar/log/fn.consistency_proof.html>`__ (fn). The consistency proof that a tree of ``old`` leaves is a prefix of these, RFC 9162 section 2.1.4. The nodes recompute both roots; a rewritten log cannot produce a set that yields both. ``None`` when ``old`` is zero or wider than the tree.
- `from_hex <rustdoc/deedar/log/fn.from_hex.html>`__ (fn). A 32 byte hash from the lower-case hex ``hex`` writes. Any other text yields nothing.
- `hex <rustdoc/deedar/log/fn.hex.html>`__ (fn). Hex, lower case, fixed width.
- `inclusion_proof <rustdoc/deedar/log/fn.inclusion_proof.html>`__ (fn). The audit path for the leaf at ``index``, RFC 9162 section 2.1.3: sibling subtree roots, innermost first.
- `leaf_hash <rustdoc/deedar/log/fn.leaf_hash.html>`__ (fn). Leaf hash, ``SHA-256(0x00 || entry)``.
- `node_hash <rustdoc/deedar/log/fn.node_hash.html>`__ (fn). Interior hash, ``SHA-256(0x01 || left || right)``.
- `root <rustdoc/deedar/log/fn.root.html>`__ (fn). The Merkle root over ``leaves``, RFC 9162 section 2.1: the tree splits at the largest power of two below the width, which is the shape the proofs below are sound over.
- `verify_consistency <rustdoc/deedar/log/fn.verify_consistency.html>`__ (fn). Whether the tree of ``old`` leaves with ``old_root`` is a prefix of the tree of ``new`` leaves with ``new_root``; both roots are recomputed from ``nodes``.
- `verify_inclusion <rustdoc/deedar/log/fn.verify_inclusion.html>`__ (fn). Whether ``leaf`` sits at ``index`` of a tree of ``size`` leaves with that root.
